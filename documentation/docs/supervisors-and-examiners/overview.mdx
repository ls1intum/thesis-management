# Supervisors & Examiners

This section is written for teaching staff who run the thesis process end-to-end
— creating topics, reviewing applications, running interview rounds,
supervising running theses, and (as an examiner) issuing the final grade.

## Where to start

- Read the **[Supervisor & Examiner Guide](./supervisor-examiner-guide)** for
  the full walkthrough. The two roles are covered together, with Examiner-only
  actions clearly marked.
- The **[Permissions](./permissions)** page has the full role-by-role
  permission matrix for the thesis page and research group actions.
- The **[Workflows](./workflows)** page has visual flowcharts of the
  application and thesis-writing processes.

## Terminology

The UI uses two staff role labels:

- **Supervisor** — day-to-day advisor of a thesis. Creates topics, reviews
  applications, gives feedback on proposal and thesis.
- **Examiner** — the responsible professor. Can do everything a Supervisor can
  do, and additionally sets the **final grade** and **completes** the thesis.

Behind the scenes the Keycloak groups are still called `advisor` (= Supervisor)
and `supervisor` (= Examiner) for historical reasons — this only matters if you
look at log files or configuration.
