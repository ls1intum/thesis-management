# Students

Welcome. This section is written for students who want to use the Thesis
Management platform end-to-end — from browsing open topics through submitting an
application, booking an interview slot, writing your thesis, and receiving your
final grade.

## Where to start

- Read the **[Student Guide](./student-guide)** for the full walkthrough. It is
  structured so you can jump straight to the step you need instead of reading
  cover-to-cover.

## Terminology

The platform uses two staff role labels you will encounter in the UI:

- **Supervisor** — your day-to-day advisor. Creates topics, reviews
  applications, gives feedback on your proposal and thesis.
- **Examiner** — the responsible professor. Can do everything a Supervisor can
  do, and additionally sets the **final grade** and **completes** the thesis.
