The Research Group for Applied Education Technologies (referred to as AET in the following paragraphs) from the Technical University of Munich takes the protection of private data seriously. We process personal data collected when you visit and use our application, in compliance with the applicable data protection regulations, in particular the Bavarian Data Protection Act (BayDSG), the Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Unless otherwise stated, the legal basis for processing your data is Art. 6(1)(e) GDPR in conjunction with Art. 4(1) BayDSG (performance of a task carried out in the public interest). For the organization and assessment of theses as examination performances, Art. 84 of the Bavarian Higher Education Innovation Act (BayHIG) and the General Examination and Study Regulations (Allgemeine Prüfungs- und Studienordnung, APSO) of the Technical University of Munich additionally apply. Below, we inform you about the type, scope and purpose of the collection and use of personal data.

Purposes and legal bases for processing

The purpose of the processing is to organize and assess theses within the university environment, including the application, supervision, presentation, and grading processes. The following legal bases apply to the processing described in this statement:

  • Art. 6(1)(e) GDPR in conjunction with Art. 4(1) BayDSG (performance of a task carried out in the public interest) for the general operation of the application and the organization and assessment of theses;
  • Art. 84 BayHIG and the General Examination and Study Regulations (APSO) of the Technical University of Munich for the processing of examination-related data, in particular theses, assessments, and grades;
  • Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent) for data you provide voluntarily, in particular optional profile information, uploaded documents, and the import of a profile picture from Gravatar; and
  • Art. 6(1)(e) GDPR in conjunction with Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG for information that is stored on your device and is technically necessary to operate the application.

Where a processing operation relies on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), you may withdraw that consent at any time (see Your rights).

Recipients of personal data

Your personal data may be accessible to the following recipients within the application:

  • Supervisors and advisors of the research group you are associated with can view your profile, application data, uploaded documents, and thesis information.
  • Administrators can view and manage all data in the application.
  • Other students cannot view your personal data unless explicitly shared through the thesis process (e.g. public thesis presentations).
  • Public visitors (unauthenticated): The following data is publicly accessible without requiring a user account:
    • Completed thesis listings with public visibility, including thesis title, type, abstract, start and end dates, and the names of students, supervisors, and examiners. Only theses whose visibility is set to "public" by a supervisor appear in this listing.
    • Thesis presentation schedules whose visibility is set to "public", including presentation dates, locations, stream links, and the names of participants.
    • Published thesis topic listings, including topic title and the names of supervisors and examiners.
    • Research group information, including the name and contact details of the research group head.
    • Profile pictures (avatars) of users who appear in publicly visible data (e.g. published theses, topics, or research group listings).

Data is not transferred to recipients outside the university, except:

  • Email recipients - presentation invitations may be sent to external email addresses provided by supervisors.
  • Configured notification addresses - research groups may configure an additional notification email address that receives copies of new-application emails, including attached documents (e.g. CV, examination reports) when the research group has enabled the inclusion of application data in emails. These addresses may be external to the university.
  • Gravatar (Automattic Inc., USA) - when you choose to import a profile picture (see below).

Duration of storage

Personal data is stored only for as long as is necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this statement, taking into account statutory retention periods. The specific retention periods for the individual processing operations are set out in the section Data retention and deletion below.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR): You have the right to request information about the personal data we process about you. You can use the data export feature in the application settings to download a copy of your data.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR): You have the right to request the correction of inaccurate personal data. Some data (name, email, matriculation number) is synchronized from your university account and must be corrected there.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR): You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data. You can delete your account in the application settings. Voluntarily provided profile data and rejected application data will be deleted promptly upon request. Thesis-related data (including accepted applications, assessments, and grades) is subject to mandatory retention under university examination regulations and cannot be deleted until the retention period has expired. In this case, your account will be deactivated and non-essential data will be deleted immediately, while thesis-related data will be fully deleted after the retention period expires.
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR): You have the right to request the restriction of processing of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR): You have the right to receive the personal data you have provided to us in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format. You can use the data export feature in the application settings to download your data as a ZIP file containing JSON-formatted data and your uploaded files.
  • Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data based on Art. 6(1)(e) GDPR at any time, for reasons arising from your particular situation.
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR): You can revoke any consent you have given at any time. An informal message by email is sufficient. The lawfulness of the data processing carried out on the basis of the consent until the revocation remains unaffected.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at ls1.admin@in.tum.de.

Information about the web presence

SSL/TLS encryption

For security reasons and to protect the transmission of confidential content, this application uses SSL/TLS encryption. This means that data you transmit via this application cannot be read by third parties. You can recognize an encrypted connection by the "https://" address line in your browser and by the lock symbol in the browser line.

Server logging

The web servers are operated by the AET. Every time the application is accessed, the web server temporarily processes the following information in log files:

  • IP address of the requesting computer
  • Date and time of access
  • Name, URL, and transferred data volume of the accessed resource
  • Access status (requested resource transferred, not found, etc.)
  • Identification data of the browser and operating system used (if transmitted by the requesting web browser)
  • Website from which access was made (if transmitted by the requesting web browser)

The log entries can be continuously and automatically evaluated in order to detect attacks on the web servers and react accordingly. In individual cases, i.e. in the event of reported malfunctions, errors and security incidents, a manual analysis may be carried out.

Retention period: Log files are automatically deleted after 90 days, unless they are needed to investigate a specific security incident.

Authentication and session data

Authentication is handled through Keycloak (Single Sign-On) using your university credentials. The application does not store your password. Authentication tokens (JWT) are stored in your browser's local storage for the duration of your session. The following data is stored in your browser:

  • Authentication tokens (in localStorage, cleared on logout)
  • Privacy consent status (in localStorage)
  • User interface preferences (in localStorage)

Information on individual processing operations

User account and profile data

When you log in to this application via your university account (Single Sign-On via Keycloak), the following personal data is retrieved from your university identity provider:

  • First name and last name
  • Email address
  • University ID (username)
  • Matriculation number (if available)

In addition, the application itself stores and manages the following account data:

  • Your role assignments within this application (student, supervisor, advisor, administrator)
  • Last login timestamp
  • Account creation and last profile update timestamps

Role assignments are maintained within this application (in its own database) and are not taken from your identity provider, so that changes to your roles take effect immediately. The authentication service (Keycloak) is operated by the university itself on its own infrastructure. No passwords are stored or processed by this application.

In addition, you may voluntarily provide the following information in your user profile:

  • Gender
  • Nationality
  • Study degree and study program
  • Enrollment semester
  • Interests, projects, and special skills (free text)
  • Additional profile fields configured centrally for this application instance

Your membership in a research group is assigned by administrators and is not part of the information you provide yourself.

Retention period: See Data retention and deletion.

Uploaded documents

As part of the thesis application process, you may upload the following documents:

  • Curriculum vitae (CV)
  • Examination report
  • Degree report (for Master's students)
  • Profile picture (avatar)

During the thesis process, additional documents may be uploaded, such as thesis proposals, final thesis submissions, and supporting files. With the exception of profile pictures, these documents are stored on the application server and are accessible only to you and to authorized personnel (supervisors, advisors, and administrators of the relevant research group). Profile pictures (avatars) are handled differently: they are publicly accessible for users who appear in publicly visible data (see Recipients of personal data).

Retention period: Uploaded documents are retained for the same period as the associated thesis or application data (see Data retention and deletion).

Profile picture lookup (Gravatar)

If you choose to import a profile picture from Gravatar, the application sends a SHA-256 hash of your email address to the Gravatar service operated by Automattic Inc. (USA) at gravatar.com. Only the cryptographic hash is transmitted, not your email address itself. The request is made server-side so that your IP address is not exposed to Gravatar. No data is sent to Gravatar unless you explicitly trigger the profile picture import.

International transfer: Automattic Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. For details, see Automattic's privacy policy.

Thesis and application data

When you apply for or work on a thesis, the following data is processed:

  • Thesis application details (motivation text, desired start date, preferred thesis topic)
  • Thesis metadata (title, type, abstract, keywords, language)
  • Assessment and grading data (feedback, grade suggestions, final grade)
  • Comments and communication within the thesis process
  • Presentation details (date, time, location, stream link)
  • Interview scheduling data (time slots, location, stream link) and interview assessment notes

Retention period: See Data retention and deletion.

Email notifications

The application sends automated email notifications related to the thesis process, including:

  • Application status updates (submission confirmation, acceptance, rejection)
  • Application review reminders for research group members
  • Interview invitations, scheduling confirmations, and booking updates
  • Thesis creation and lifecycle notifications
  • Thesis proposal submission, acceptance, and revision request notifications
  • Presentation scheduling notifications and invitations
  • Comment notifications
  • Assessment and final grade notifications
  • Data export readiness notifications
  • Thesis data anonymization reminders (sent to research group heads)

These emails may contain personal data such as your name, thesis title, and relevant process details. Some emails include file attachments (e.g. CV, examination report, or thesis proposal documents). The research group head receives a copy (BCC) of application acceptance and rejection emails as well as thesis creation and closure notifications. Additionally, research groups may configure an additional notification email address that receives a copy of every new application; if the research group has enabled the inclusion of application data in emails, this copy also contains the attached documents (such as CV and examination reports), otherwise it contains only a link to the application. You can configure your preferences for certain notification types in the application settings.

Calendar subscription feeds

The application provides calendar subscription feeds (ICS format) that users can subscribe to in their calendar applications (e.g. Outlook, Google Calendar, Apple Calendar). These feeds contain:

  • Presentation calendar: Thesis titles, presentation dates, locations or stream links, and names of participants for thesis presentations.
  • Interview calendar: Interview time slots, locations or stream links, and topic titles for individual users.

These calendar feeds are accessible without authentication. The interview feed is addressed by a non-guessable user identifier; the presentation feed is addressed by the research group's public abbreviation and contains only presentations that have been marked as public.

Data export

You can request an export of your personal data in the application settings. The export is generated as a ZIP file containing your data in JSON format together with your uploaded files (e.g. CV, examination report, degree report, and profile picture). This feature implements your right of access (Art. 15 GDPR) and your right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR).

Retention period: Export files are available for download for 7 days after generation, after which they are automatically deleted.

Data retention and deletion

Personal data is stored for the duration necessary to fulfill the purposes described above:

  • User account data: Student accounts that have been inactive for more than one year are automatically disabled. Logging in again reactivates a disabled account. The profile data of a disabled account is deleted when you request deletion of your account; thesis-related data follows the retention periods described below.
  • Thesis data and accepted applications: Thesis data, including assessment and grading records and the associated application, is retained for 5 years after the end of the calendar year of the latest thesis activity (completion date, last state change, or creation date), in accordance with university examination regulations. After this retention period expires, thesis data is anonymized automatically: personal references, files, comments, assessments, and feedback are removed, while the thesis title, type, grade, and dates are preserved for statistical purposes.
  • Rejected application data: Applications that are rejected are retained for 1 year after rejection to allow for inquiries and reapplications, after which they are automatically deleted.
  • Unassessed application data: Applications that have not been reviewed are deleted when the associated user account is deleted.
  • Uploaded documents: Retained for the same period as the associated thesis or application data.
  • Data export files: Available for download for 7 days after generation, after which they are automatically deleted.
  • Server log files: Automatically deleted after 90 days.

The automated data lifecycle processing described above (account deactivation, application deletion, thesis anonymization) runs as scheduled tasks. Affected research group heads receive advance notification before thesis data is anonymized.

Email security

If you email us, your email address will only be used for correspondence with you. Please note that data transmission on the Internet can have security gaps. Complete protection of data from access by third parties is not possible.
